1967
Landmark Work

Neisser’s 1967 book helped establish cognitive psychology as a distinct field.

Neisser’s Definition of Cognition

"The processes through which sensory information is transformed, reduced, elaborated, stored, recovered, and used."

Step 1

1. Transforming

Converting sensory input into meaningful info

Step 2

2. Reducing

Filtering information through attention

Step 3

3. Elaborating

Connecting new info with existing knowledge

Step 4

4. Storing & Recovering

Retaining & recalling info through memory

Step 5

5. Using

Applying info to make decisions & solve problems

Statement #3

"Blind people experience a drastic, superhuman sharpening of their physical sense of hearing."

Ask students to vote: Is this a FACT or a MYTH?

IT'S A MYTH!

The Scientific Fact:

Their physical hearing mechanics are identical to sighted people; instead, the brain repurposes the unused visual cortex to process auditory information, allowing for superior cognitive processing of echoes and sound locations.
